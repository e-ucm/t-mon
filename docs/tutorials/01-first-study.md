# Tutorial: First Study in T-Mon

## Objective

Get your first study up and running in T-Mon, from logging in to viewing
visualizations.

## Step 1: Log In

1. Open T-Mon in your browser
2. Click "Login" or "Sign in"
3. Complete the Keycloak/OIDC authentication flow
4. You should see "Logged in as [your username]"

## Step 2: Select a Study

1. Navigate to the browser section
2. You'll see a list of studies (simlets) available in your SIMVA account
3. Click on a study to select it
4. The study name and ID will be displayed

## Step 2: Run Analysis

1. After selecting a study, you'll see options to run analysis
2. Click "Run Analysis" or the analysis button
3. Wait for the analysis to complete
4. The system will process the xAPI-SG statements

## Step 3: View Visualizations

1. After analysis completes, navigate to the desired tab
2. Available tabs may include:
   - Home Page
   - Progress
   - Videos
   - Completables
   - And more
3. Each tab displays different visualizations of your study data

## Step 4: Explore Further

1. Try different tabs to see various visualizations
2. Use the filter options to narrow down your view
3. Save or export your findings if needed

## Step 4: Log Out

1. When finished, click "Logout" to end your session

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Can't log in | Check your Keycloak/OIDC credentials |
| No studies available | Ensure you have studies in your SIMVA account |
| Analysis fails | Check console for error messages |
| Tiles not appearing | Verify studies are loaded and you're on the right tab |
